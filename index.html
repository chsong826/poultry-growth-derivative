🐓 Broiler Chicken Study & Data Origin

This simulation uses commercial performance data collected from Ross 308 Broiler Chickens raised in a modern tunnel-ventilated closed-house poultry farm located in Gopeng, Perak, Malaysia.

The function f(x) calculates cumulative body weight, while the derivative f'(x) calculates the exact daily gain rate.

Chicken Weight Function
f(x) = A × e-e-B(x-x*)
Daily Gain Derivative
f'(x) = A × B × e-B(x-x*) - e-B(x-x*)
📊 Calculus Parameters
• x = Age (days)
• x* = Inflection Point
• A = Mature Weight
• B = Growth Velocity
• k = Genetic Coefficient
• η = Climate Efficiency

🇲🇾 Malaysian Poultry Science

In Malaysia’s tropical climate, heat stress severely impacts broiler performance. High temperatures reduce feed intake and increase physiological stress.

Modern Malaysian poultry farms therefore utilize tunnel ventilation systems with evaporative cooling pads to maintain optimal environmental conditions.

  • Starter Phase (0–10 Days): Rapid gut and skeletal development.
  • Grower Phase (11–28 Days): Peak muscle expansion and maximum metabolic heat production.
  • Finisher Phase (29–42 Days): Growth slows while feed intake rises significantly.
  • Harvest Optimization: Past Day 42, Feed Conversion Ratio (FCR) deteriorates rapidly.
Reference benchmark: Malaysian Society of Animal Production (MSAP) standards for Ross 308 tropical housing systems.
